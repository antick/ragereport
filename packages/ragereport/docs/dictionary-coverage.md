# English and Hindi dictionary coverage

Updated on 05 Oct 2026 against [the built-in dictionary](../src/config/words.ts) and the built detector. Hindi entries use Latin phonetic spelling. Report headings, tier names, and messages stay in English.

The lists below include every configured swear and insult spelling, grouped by report family. The dictionary is curated, not an exhaustive list of every regional word or possible spelling.

These totals exclude politeness entries and count explicitly configured spellings, not every stretched spelling. There are 260 swear entries and 30 separate insult entries. Mild/contextual insults contribute to insult totals rather than the swear-to-politeness ratio.

A `(loose)` marker means `--loose-matching` is required. Bare names, technical abbreviations, and ambiguous short forms are guarded to limit accidental matches. Dotted forms such as `b.c.` are recognized by default.

## Matching and counting

- Whole-word matching prevents `lun` from matching `column` and `cock` from matching `cockpit`. Short words such as `lun` can still match ordinary abbreviations used as standalone words; use `ignoreWords` to exclude them if needed.
- Matching ignores case and accepts stretched letters, such as `BHADWEEE` and `fuuuuck`. It does not match arbitrary substrings.
- Multiword expressions use the longest overlapping match: `behen chod` and `randi rona` count once, rather than also counting their component words.
- Phrases such as `son of a bitch`, `holy shit`, and `fuck off` count their base swear once.
- Variants share a report family while preserving the exact matched spelling and count.
- Code is ignored unless `--include-code` is supplied. Domain/email hostnames are excluded; URL paths and ordinary sentences can still match.
- Dictionary, source, and build changes invalidate scan caches, so rebuilding updates the counts on the next scan.

## Context and remaining scope

The tool counts vocabulary rather than inferring intent. Animal names, literal bodily references, quotations, and ordinary adjectives can also appear as insults. Mild terms such as `kamina`, `gadha`, and `fattu` are tracked separately as insults. `kutte` and `suar` are enabled as swears by default.

Ordinary anatomical terms such as `penis`, `vagina`, and `breasts` are not automatically treated as swears. Identity labels and identity-targeted slurs are outside this dictionary's scope. Additional language packs remain future work; their vocabulary will not change the English report interface.

[Ofcom discusses why context matters and separates general profanity, anatomy, and discriminatory language](https://www.ofcom.org.uk/siteassets/resources/documents/research-and-data/tv-radio-and-on-demand-research/tv-research/offensive-language-quick-reference-guide.pdf?v=326908). Published blocklists are useful sources of candidates, not proof that every occurrence indicates rage.

## Verification

Automated tests cover spelling variants, default and loose matching, family grouping, phrases and their component words, code and hostname exclusions, language filters, stretched spelling offsets, report totals, and 20,000 repeated multiword matches. The source dictionary contains no duplicate spelling entries.
